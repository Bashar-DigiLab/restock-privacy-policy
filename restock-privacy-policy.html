Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Restock app ("Restock", "we", or "the app") handles information when you use it to prepare retailer restock lists, manage products and suppliers, and track shopping or purchase history.

1. Account and sign-in

Restock does not require you to create an account or sign in to use its core features.

2. Information stored on your device

Information you enter into Restock may include store details, supplier details, product names and categories, quantities, prices, restock lists, purchase status, purchase history, and notes. In the current local-storage setup, this information is stored on your device for the app's features and is not uploaded by Restock to a server operated by the app developer.

3. Information collection and sharing

Restock does not sell your locally stored restock or shopping-list information. The app does not intentionally send that information to the developer or to third parties as part of its local-storage features.

If you choose to export or share information, the resulting file or content may be saved to a location you select or passed to another app or service you choose. That destination's handling of the information is governed by its own privacy practices.

Android, your device manufacturer, or other system services may provide device-level backup or diagnostic features outside Restock's control. Check your device settings and the relevant service's privacy information for details.

4. Third-party and device services

The core restock-list features described in this policy are designed to work locally on your device. If you choose to export or share information, use device-level backup, or use another external service, that service may handle information under its own privacy policy. Restock does not control those external services.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your locally stored app data remains on your device until you delete it using an available in-app data-clearing feature, clear the app's storage through Android settings, or uninstall the app. Exported files, shared copies, and device-level backups may remain outside the app after local data is deleted.

6. Security

Restock relies on the storage and security controls provided by your device and Android. Keep your device protected with a screen lock and install system updates when available. No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's privacy

Restock is a general-purpose retailer productivity app and is not designed specifically for children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this page with its effective date.
